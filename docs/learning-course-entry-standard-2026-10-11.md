# Learning course entry standard

**Approved:** 11 October 2026. Applies to all present and future published Learning entries in Russian, Kazakh and English, including free, paid, externally hosted and Autism School Kazakhstan courses.

Every published course, module or training resource must show two **distinct** clearly written fields:

1. **How to begin learning** (`registrationStatus`): access route, registration or enrolment requirements, required assessments only if needed to start, and restrictions that actually affect access.
2. **What is issued after completion** (`completionOutcome`): specify whether a course gives a certificate of completion, statement of participation, digital badge, CPD/CEU credits or only learning materials. Explain requirements for each and distinguish a standard completion certificate from formal education credits. If the provider has not confirmed a certificate, state explicitly that certificate issuance is not confirmed.

Never guess a certificate, imply professional accreditation or imply that continuing education credit is an academic qualification. Do not use the access field to discuss recognition or equivalence in Kazakhstan. The provider may update all conditions. Cite official programme/course-level materials for every award claim; if sources conflict, report uncertainty and direct the learner to confirm with the awarding organisation.

These two fields are mandatory **at publication**, not necessarily while a new course remains an incomplete draft. The Astro content schema and `npm run check:learning-outcomes` block incomplete published records. CMS field hints reiterate these conditions.

This structural check cannot independently verify a provider's current policy; source verification and editorial review remain separate responsibilities.

Current inventory reviewed: seven courses in three catalogue languages (21 published entries), all with both fields.

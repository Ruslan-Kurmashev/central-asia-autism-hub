# Free external learning catalogue: first publication wave

**Verified:** 11 October 2026. **Section:** Learning only; no professional reference section content changed.

## Publication scope

Eight verified resources; three independent navigation-language descriptions per resource (24 metadata entries), sharing canonical official programme URLs. Original course languages are displayed on each card.

1. Kazan Federal University / Family Academy: 29 online lessons, Russian; university official page states Stepik course is free. Independent Stepik access could not be verified and the card states this.
2. The Open University / Understanding Autism: 24-hour English course; materials open without enrolment, sign in for free badge.
3. OCALI / Autism Internet Modules: 50 self-paced English modules, free module certificates, paid graduate credits optional.
4. CDC / Watch Me!: free four-module/one-hour training (English); free CDC CEU available under provider rules, without Kazakhstan qualification claims.
5. Vanderbilt IRIS / Autism (Part 1): two hours, freely readable module, free certificate with account.
6. Education Scotland / OLCreate / Introduction to Autism and Inclusive Practice: three hours, free badge with registration; Scottish context.
7. The Open University / Supporting Children's Development: 15-hour general inclusive development course.
8. Naked Heart Foundation / online learning materials: publicly accessible Russian resources, presented as an educational library rather than a course.

## Editorial boundaries

Only free, open-access learning resources are placed in this wave. Do not imply course content is translated to RU / KK by AutismHub: only the **catalogue descriptions** are translated. All programmes are run by third parties and all certificates/academic credits are governed by their issuer; neither learning nor certificates imply medical licensing in Kazakhstan.

"Au" by Anton Tut Ryadom was reviewed but excluded: the October 2026 cohort had already started and ongoing availability to join was not confirmed.

# Genetics, environment and autism: provenance of original science illustrations

Prepared on 15 October 2026 for AutismHub article `src/content/pages/kz/ru/genetics-environment-autism-risk-evidence.md`.

All three figures were created as original editable SVG markup for AutismHub. They do not use generative image models, external publisher illustrations, patient records, photographs or copied scientific graphics. Their purpose is educational explanation of evidence quality, not personalised diagnosis or risk prediction.

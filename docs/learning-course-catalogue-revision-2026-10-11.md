# Learning course catalogue: direct-course revision

**Date:** 11 October 2026

Seven discrete courses or learning modules appear in the catalogue. Editorial descriptions are in RU, KK and EN, while course language is always shown separately.

The following URLs lead to specific training:
- Family Academy: https://stepik.org/course/115174/ (direct course link given on Kazan Federal University's public announcement; participation from Kazakhstan not independently tested).
- Understanding Autism: https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/understanding-autism
- Watch Me!: https://www.cdc.gov/watch-me-training/modules/index.html
- Autism (Part 1), Educators: https://iris.peabody.vanderbilt.edu/module/asd1/
- Autism (Part 2), Evidence-Based Practices: https://iris.peabody.vanderbilt.edu/module/asd2/

- Supporting Children's Development: https://www.open.edu/openlearn/education-development/childhood-youth/supporting-childrens-development/

The broad OCALI Autism Internet Modules collection and Naked Heart Online library have been removed from course entries. They are resource libraries, not discrete course cards.

Catalogue changes: replace platform-like promotion with a neutral single-column course catalogue, text search, audience, language and topic filters, direct course calls to action, concise duration and enrolment details. Provider attribution appears solely as provenance. The redundant Materials and Topics block and bottom generic topic map are omitted in Learning only.

Availability and credential status are subject to changes by the external providers. No course has been accredited by AutismHub or guaranteed recognition in Kazakhstan.

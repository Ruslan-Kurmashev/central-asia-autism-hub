# Editorial decision: Learning versus For Professionals

**Approved:** 11 October 2026  
**Applies to:** RU, KK and EN navigation and future content.

| Visitor intent | Canonical section |
|---|---|
| Enrol, learn from lessons, attend a webinar or compare courses | Learning |
| Apply evidence, consult guidance, use a diagnostic or practice workflow | For Professionals |
| Find accessible explanations for family life | For Parents |
| Understand one published research study | Research Explained |
| Navigate Kazakhstan-specific rights, services or administration | Help in Kazakhstan |

**Publishing rules**

1. One original resource, one canonical content home, with cross-links instead of copies.
2. A course contains learning objectives, lessons and exercises; an article is not a course.
3. Courses for professionals are categorised under Learning only.
4. For Professionals contains guidance, tools, practice checklists, evidence and safety notes.
5. The old `cpd` professional topic is retired and replaced with `practice-tools`. Professional training cross-links to `learning/#professionals-learning`.
6. The existing main section paths do not change; no redirects needed for those paths.
7. Three-language terminology should remain semantically equivalent and receive a native-language editorial review before a final language-quality signoff.
8. No new training programme or professional material is represented as already published by these architecture changes.

**Next content milestone:** build distinct professional references and self-contained course programmes under these two taxonomies.

## Changes

- Distinguished functional behaviour assessment (FBA) from eight subsequent learning/support procedures. FBA remains described in its own H2 and is no longer duplicated and misleadingly numbered as an intervention.
- Reorganised introductory explanation into communication/environment and skill-building options, with explicit warning that the eight methods are not a prescribed sequential programme.
- Removed the stray Kazakh-language word "Маман" by eliminating the duplicate FBA text.
- Added safeguards against using high-probability request sequences to override refusal or create coercion.
- Added accessible everyday examples for differential reinforcement, prompting, task analysis, video modelling, without changing general evidence claims.
- Clarified that a child may refuse a group activity for reasons other than overload, and that functional assessment is not a technique to compel participation.
- Removed unused systematic review on exercises and stereotypy (Wong et al. 2023) from structured source metadata rather than importing an unsupported claim into the text.
- Updated description and updatedAt to 2026-11-24. Original publication date preserved.

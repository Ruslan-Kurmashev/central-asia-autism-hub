# Kazakhstan social rights and supports: scoped correction, 2026-11-24

## Scope
Updated RU, EN, KK versions of `help-social-support-rights-kazakhstan`. Original publication dates, slugs, translation keys, image, existing review states and next review due dates remain unchanged.

## Material changes
- Consolidated MSE Rules under Order 260 were amended by Order 406 of 15 October 2026: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2600040183. The general four-month referral rule includes specified exceptions. Autism alone does not establish an exception.
- Ministry of Labour clarification confirms IPAR amendments can also be processed remotely and proactively for all diagnoses: https://www.gov.kz/org/enbek-aktobe/press/news/details/1331163?lang=ru.
- Explained MSE conclusion appeal: three months from receipt to file, a general 20 working-day administrative review period, secure electronic route through eOtinish: https://www.gov.kz/org/enbek-zko/press/news/details/1311609?lang=ru and https://egov.kz/cms/ru/services/citizen_and_the_government/e_app.
- Added independently date-marked 2026 parental allowance of 81,871 tenge per month, not to be projected into 2027: https://www.gov.kz/org/enbek-aktobe/press/news/details/1334844?lang=ru.
- Removed duplicate in-body list of official sources because ArticleLayout renders structured source metadata.
- Added correct EN and KK links to existing parent explainer on PMPK, VKK and MSE.
- Added three new `pending` claims to `docs/evidence/claims-register.csv`, no new human verification claim.

## Limitations
This is AI-assisted source review. Independent legal, medical and native-language expert sign-off has not been conducted. The October 2026 historical review tracker is not marked freshly completed. Neither MSE status nor benefit eligibility can be determined from an autism diagnosis alone.

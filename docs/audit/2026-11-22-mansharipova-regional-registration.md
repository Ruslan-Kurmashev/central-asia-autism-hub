## Source
Mansharipova Z. et al. 2026, Healthcare 14(22), 3910. https://doi.org/10.3390/healthcare14223910. Published 14 November 2026. Primary indexed publisher abstract, results, tables, methods and version notes consulted. Ecological analysis of Form 10-f, new registrations under codes F84.0 / F84.1 / F84.5, ages 0-17, 2015-2025.

## Checked results
- National first-registration rate: 3.9 to 63.5 per 100,000 children (2015 and 2025).
- Astana 2025 first registration rate: 221.4 per 100,000. Akmola: 119.5. Karaganda including Ulytau: 28.7.
- Child psychiatry density: 2.06 to 1.43 per 100,000 children. Concurrent trends, not a causal workforce effect.
- 16 historical regional units. For 2015-2022, regional child denominator sums were 7-9% below the national sum. Administrative-border changes required additional adjustments. Some rates remain uncertain.
- Factcheck.kz / Ministry of Health Astana figure 712.9 per 100,000 is for children registered under a different administrative measure, not new registrations. Never combine these two values as if they are one metric.
- The new study does not measure population prevalence, diagnostic waiting times, or causal drivers of different rates. Study sample, measurements and boundaries differ from existing Frontiers research.

# Stage 4, batch 2: selective Kazakhstan support article audit

Audit date: 2026-10-26
Scope: four article groups, 12 published RU/KK/EN pages
Status: targeted primary-source review only. Not an independent legal, medical or language audit.

## Findings by group

1. `help-kazakhstan-start` (RU/KK/EN). The official PMPK state-service rules, document V2000020744, clause 7 as amended 26 June 2026, distinguish a waiting time of up to 30 calendar days for assessment. The gov.kz service card covers several different services and separately lists a 90-365 day period for rehabilitation/support. All three page versions now clarify that the 30-day period does not guarantee commencement of any support service. Original `publishedAt` was retained.
2. `help-autism-school-kazakhstan` (RU/KK/EN). Official order 92 on psychological and pedagogical support (V2500036047) has a revised edition dated 29 May 2026 under order 144-NQ. The source document V2200029329 is order 385 of 31 August **2022**, rather than 2017. Corrected the erroneous `publicationYear` metadata in all three versions. The 2026-2027 first-grade schedule is already clearly identified as historical and not transferable to later years.
3. `help-development-concerns-kazakhstan` (RU/KK/EN). Reviewed the distinctions between medical evaluation and PMPK, emergency symptoms and non-diagnostic screening language. No change was justified in this limited review. Podiatric standard current amendments and local service pathways require a deeper review.
4. `help-verify-specialist-center-kazakhstan` (RU/KK/EN). Law 332-VIII dated 2 July 2026, Articles 3, 13, 21 and 22, confirms a one-year psychologist-registration transition after commencement, and deferred certification Article 17 to 1 January 2028. The existing versions already describe this correctly. No individual licence or registry entry was verified.

## Scientific source comparison and limits

- Crompton et al. (2025), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02163-z and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40369213/: publisher text and PubMed confirm 311 adults (154 autistic, 157 non-autistic), 54 chains, 324 recruited and the comparison with 72 participants in the earlier study. Retain the distinction between measured information transfer and participant-rated rapport. Lack of statistical significance does not establish equal communication abilities in every setting. Retain the unmodified credited original Figure 3 and CC BY 4.0 link.
- Velmeshev et al. (2019), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31097668/ and https://genome.bio.fsu.edu/cgi-bin/hgTrackUi?db=hg38&g=cortexVelmeshev: primary abstract, figures and dataset description support upper-layer neuronal and microglial changes, 15 autistic donors and 16 controls, 41 cortical samples and 104,559 profiles. The published text as indexed also reports 52,003 and 52,556 profiles and the additional epilepsy/control comparison. Preserve the donor-versus-nucleus distinction, the observational design and the restriction against diagnostic or treatment claims. The existing rat cell-culture photograph is not an image of a human autism brain sample.
- McQuaid et al. (2024), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38587289/ and https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13623613241243117: abstract confirms three cohorts (193, 1,550 and 244) and differences in diagnostic timing in two cohorts. Indexed primary results confirm GENDAAR means 6.25 and 5.58 years (not significant) and CNH means 8.67 and 7.41 years (significant). Other exact gender-diversity means (13.01, 7.33, 16.73 and 12.73 years) were preserved from the earlier approved Russian explainer, but could not be independently extracted again during this release because the PMC full-text request returned a browser verification page. Do not read the observed differences as causal effects of masking or gender identity.

This is a targeted source and translation consistency review, not comprehensive claim-by-claim revalidation or retraction surveillance. Historical original source-check dates remain unchanged. First publication display dates remain 2026-10-10. The EN/KK release date is 2026-11-21.

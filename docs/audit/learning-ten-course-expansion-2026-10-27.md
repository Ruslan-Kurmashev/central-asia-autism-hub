# Learning catalogue expansion, 27 October 2026

## Scope

Ten newly requested external free-to-learn educational resources were added as 30 separate RU, KK and EN catalogue description files. Neither teaching languages nor provider course contents were translated. The language shown in the catalogue is the actual provider language: four Russian and six English.

## Source and free-access review

Official organisation pages checked in this session:
- https://pedagog-course.autism.help/ (20 open lectures, audio, slides)
- https://circa.educ.ubc.ca/autism-and-neurodiversity-in-early-childcare/ (free Canvas course)
- https://circa.educ.ubc.ca/autism-and-neurodiversity-in-primary-care/ (free only through 19 November 2026, reviewDueAt set 18 November)
- https://circa.educ.ubc.ca/autism-and-neurodiversity-in-dentistry/ (free Canvas course)
- https://circa.educ.ubc.ca/autism-in-the-workplace/ (free Canvas course)
- https://reframing-autism-s-school.teachable.com/p/autism-essentials (core free, optional paid certificate)
- https://thompsoncenter.missouri.edu/autism-training-modules/ (five open modules recorded 2015-2016)
- https://rehab-portal.ru/communication-in-early-intervention (free portal mini-course)
- https://rehab-portal.ru/social-everyday-adaptation (free portal mini-course)
- https://rehab-portal.ru/introduction-to-assistive-technologies (free portal mini-course)

Caveat: the assistant's webpage viewer could not directly fetch the pedagogy and two rehabilitation pages, but web search returned the official site's indexed content. The pages were not tested by creating live accounts in Kazakhstan. Requirements may change.

## Cross-language content check

All 30 files keep a stable translationKey across languages and share the same topic, programme URL, provider, number of lessons and learning language. Summaries, access requirements and completion outcomes were compared against the original provider description. The actual original English or Russian source language is not confused with the catalogue interface language. Translations were AI-assisted and reviewed against the source claims within this preparation process, but **no independent native-language proofreader, professional reviewer, or external clinical review has been represented or claimed**.

The UBC dentistry restraint content is not described as a recommended clinical protocol. The Thompson Center record explicitly identifies its historical 2015-2016 material as potentially outdated. Canadian regulations and educational credit claims are not presented as valid Kazakhstan rules. The optional paid certificate for Reframing Autism is clearly distinguished from free self-study.

## Publication and QA

Each record has accessible registration and completion conditions, sourced provider URL, last verification date and explicit review date. This document is an editorial comparison record, not evidence of a native speaker sign-off or an independent medical audit.

The script `scripts/check-learning-language-filter.mjs` is extended to check the exact ten new programmes, actual teaching language, one card per course, time-limited free notice, and an expected 51 cards per locale after the previously approved grouping of bilingual Өрлеу programmes.

# Seven free course catalogue translations: source-to-target audit

Date: 2026-10-27
Scope: seven existing Russian-source learning records, with seven Kazakh and seven English catalogue descriptions.

## Check performed

Compared the 14 descriptions to the corresponding Russian records field by field:
- source course identity and URL, programme provider and original teaching language (Russian)
- duration, audience, topic, free access, registration restrictions and certificate statements
- Kazakhstan applicability warnings, paid ABA&Psy credential distinction, and IРAB project participation limits
- identical translation keys, original publication and displayed dates
- non-diagnostic educational wording and external-resource disclaimer

One Kazakh course title was edited for idiomatic wording: `daryn-russian-inclusive-education`.

No new training-language claim has been added. Kazakh and English are languages of the AutismHub *catalogue descriptions*, not languages of these external courses. The source course pages remain Russian.

## Review boundaries

The comparison and language polishing were AI-assisted. This log documents content and metadata consistency work, **not** independent professional/native-language review, medical validation, or verification that the providers currently offer every course. The provider conditions were taken from already-published Russian records last checked 2026-10-27, with no fabricated fresh access checks. The owner explicitly requested release of these catalogue descriptions on 2026-10-27. Subsequent feedback from Kazakh-speaking readers should be incorporated as corrections if needed.

Publication preserves original first-publication date in `originalPublishedAt` and `displayPublishedAt`, with the localized entry's first public date recorded in `publishedAt`.

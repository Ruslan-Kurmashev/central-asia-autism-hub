## Updates
- Consolidated 30 H2 sections into nine H2 and seven H3 subsections.
- Strengthened upfront and in-body warnings on head/neck coverage, entrapment in removable cover, breathing, temperature and freedom of movement, ability to remove independently, supervision, avoiding restraints and removing blanket after falling asleep.
- Clarified CPSC Pillowfort 2022 product-specific recall after two reported child asphyxiation deaths. The example does not imply quantified risk across all weighted products.
- Clarified RCOT manufacturer 10-percent guideline is neither an effective treatment dose nor a proof of safety, especially for other age groups, designs and medical conditions.
- Separated the 73 randomized from 67 analyzed in Gringras et al. (2014).
- Included Zhao et al. (2024) insomnia sensitivity analysis limited to two homogeneous trials, without transferring psychiatric-population results to autistic children.
- Retained caution about weighted vest evidence, infant safe sleep and parent/child preferences versus clinical efficacy.
- Replaced broken backward reference to draft sensory-diet article with verified published references.

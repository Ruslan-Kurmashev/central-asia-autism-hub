# Research biology translation release - wave 2 (19 November 2026)

## Release decision

The project owner explicitly authorised publication of all eight English and Kazakh translations in the AutismHub conversation on 2026-11-19. This authorisation concerns public release of the named content, not independent medical or professional language peer review.

The articles were initially drafted with AI assistance. Structure, source URLs, numerical statements, medical caution text, original publication dates, translation keys and the visual translations were compared against the Russian originals. This check does not independently authenticate all underlying research claims or replace human specialist linguistic review. No external reviewer is asserted or recorded.

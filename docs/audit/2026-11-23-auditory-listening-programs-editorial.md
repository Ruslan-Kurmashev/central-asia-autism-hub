## Changes
- Consolidated 20 H2 sections into nine H2, removed repeated adaptation material and strengthened safeguards against coerced sound exposure.
- Corrected presentation of Cochrane CD003681: last search September 2010, bibliographic update in December 2011, Cochrane current page dated 2022. Retained seven studies with 182 participants and limited findings.
- Added ASHA official 2004 position statement and retained NICE CG170 recommendation against AIT for speech-language problems.
- Preserved 2018 Chinese AIT meta-analysis and noted heterogeneous control conditions and outcomes.
- Clarified the open Tomatis RCT (n=146) studied Tomatis, hyperbaric oxygen, combination and control, and that NICE explicitly recommends against hyperbaric oxygen to treat autism. Combination results are not independent Tomatis effectiveness evidence.
- Clarified 2025 review pooled eight papers and six heterogeneous sound-based interventions among mixed pediatric populations.
- Added limitations of 55-study 2026 MMN/MMF review: observational auditory processing findings do not validate a specific commercial treatment.
- Preserved published internal page routes and original date. Did not add AI-generated cover, external-review or evidence verified claims.

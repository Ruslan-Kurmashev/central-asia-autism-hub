## Boundaries and cross-language consistency

- Each course is represented once per catalogue locale, with course teaching languages en/en/en/en/ru.
- RU, KK and EN catalogue texts describe identical provider URLs, duration, audience, conditions for accessing content, certificate fee and known limits.
- ASI professional certification or efficacy of specific techniques is not implied. The Care Learning use of 'SPD' is not represented as a universal stand-alone diagnosis. US and UK rules are not treated as Kazakhstan rules.
- The course's promotional wording is not treated as a scientific efficacy conclusion, and the full lesson content of the Russian course has not been independently audited.
- AI-assisted cross-language source/target comparison was performed. This is a consistency check, not independent native-speaker or clinical peer review. No third-party reviewer is claimed.
- Existing original content dates and original course links were not changed.

## Verification gate

The course filter regression verifies five links once each on all three catalogue languages, correct original teaching language, access and completion notes, the RUB 380 fee, 30-day free access, and a total of 56 unique displayed cards per locale, including previously grouped bilingual courses.

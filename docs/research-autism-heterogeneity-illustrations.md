# Autism heterogeneity: original scientific figures and provenance

Prepared on 15 October 2026 for AutismHub Russian article `src/content/pages/kz/ru/why-autism-differs-between-people.md`, in `Биология и механизмы аутизма`.

The three SVGs are original editable vector diagrams drawn for AutismHub. No generative image models, human photography, research participant data, identifiable cases, copyrighted journal diagrams, clinical assessments or commercially licensed stock art were used.

| File | Intended educational purpose | Interpretation limits |

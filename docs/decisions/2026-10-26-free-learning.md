# Decision: curated courses must be free to learn

- Date: 2026-10-26
- Status: approved editorial policy
- Scope: external course catalogue / Learning
- Supersedes: prior permissive treatment of paid catalogue listings in `docs/04-editorial-evidence-medical-safety.md`

Publish only external courses with complete educational material available without required payment. Verify organiser, active URL, sign-up requirements, availability, learning languages, price conditions, and any certificate fees. A paid optional certificate may be described only when the full learning material itself remains free. Reject trials with later mandatory payment, paid core lessons, and unverifiable free claims. Review access conditions periodically.

This policy does not automatically ban every paid event announcement elsewhere on the website. Announcements have a separate curation process and must clearly disclose price.

Updated authoritative documents: `docs/04-editorial-evidence-medical-safety.md`, `docs/03-information-architecture.md`, and `AGENTS.md`.

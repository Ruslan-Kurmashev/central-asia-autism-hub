# Kazakhstan medical and legal source audit - 10 October 2026

Scope: initial high-risk editorial pass across Kazakhstan Help (KZ-HLP-001 through KZ-HLP-008) and the Russian parent articles on medical diagnosis, autism assessments, PMPK/VKK/MSE, early follow-up and family social support. Three language variants have been updated where shared source findings required corrections.

This review checks traceability and specific time-sensitive legal statements against published primary sources. It is **not** an independent legal opinion, clinical peer review, or a claim that every external link or every one of the 70 parent articles was exhaustively verified.

## Substantive observations retained

- Medical ASD diagnosis, education-related assessment by PMPK and disability determination by MSE have distinct functions. A diagnosis by itself does not automatically grant disability status.
- ADOS-2, MRI, EEG and broad genetic testing should not be presented as universal mandatory requirements for every child. Their role must be clinically justified.
- There is no universal rule requiring every family to complete medical diagnosis, then PMPK, then VKK, then MSE in that exact sequence.
- Claims about guaranteed therapy results, mandatory paid programmes, or automatically required private services should be avoided.
- Official service eligibility, local procedures, personal entitlements and case-specific decisions require confirmation with the competent authority.

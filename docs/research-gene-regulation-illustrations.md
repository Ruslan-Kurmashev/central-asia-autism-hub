# Original AutismHub gene-regulation diagrams

Created for the Russian draft article "Как гены управляют развитием мозга?", 15 October 2026.

These three SVG assets were hand-authored as source code for the AutismHub educational resource. No image generation model, borrowed publisher figures, photographs of people, biomedical test data, or external fonts are used.

| Image | Location | Editorial meaning and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `autism-gene-regulation-cover.svg` | `public/images/research/` | Abstract combination of DNA and a neuron-like cell. It is not a microscopy image, an experimentally observed neuron or an autism biomarker. |
| `gene-expression-brain-development.svg` | `public/images/research/` | Simplified flow for protein-coding genes: DNA, mRNA via transcription, protein via translation and cellular functions. It does not imply every gene codes for a protein or a direct gene-to-diagnosis pathway. |
| `chromatin-accessibility-explained.svg` | `public/images/research/` | Accessible vs relatively compact chromatin as a conceptual comparison. This is not binary on/off regulation; DNA methylation and histone marks have context-dependent effects. |

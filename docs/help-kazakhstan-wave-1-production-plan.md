# Help in Kazakhstan - Wave 1 production plan

Status: production preparation complete; all eight content files remain `draft: true`.

## Editorial principles

- This section answers practical Kazakhstan navigation questions rather than repeating the general autism/intervention library.
- Official Kazakhstan sources establish legal, regulatory and state-service facts.
- Provider websites must not be used as proof of licence, professional compliance or efficacy.
- A licence, diploma, certificate or legal availability does not prove that a method is effective for autism.
- Implementation-sensitive facts are rechecked immediately before publication.
- Exact benefit amounts, school-year dates, service availability and digital-service workflows are dated and refreshed rather than written as permanent facts.
- Existing Parent-section articles are cross-linked instead of duplicated.

## Deliberate non-duplication

The previously proposed page "Поликлиника, психиатр, ПМПК, ВКК и МСЭ: кто за что отвечает" is not recreated in this section because a dedicated Parent-section article already covers that question:

`/kz/ru/parents/autism-pmpk-vkk-mse-kazakhstan/`

Wave 1 instead adds two complementary navigation pages:
- where to start across systems;
- where to go when development raises concern.

Likewise, the existing Parent-section article on choosing a specialist remains the method/fit article, while KZ-HLP-003 focuses specifically on Kazakhstan legal scope, licence and qualification verification.

## Required pre-publication QA for every Wave 1 page

1. Verify every legal/regulatory statement against the current official source.
2. Confirm that no repealed education rule is cited as current.
3. Date all implementation-sensitive claims.
4. Keep medical, educational and social routes distinct.
5. Do not imply that autism diagnosis automatically grants disability status, benefits, a school placement or a specific support package.
6. Do not infer provider compliance from advertising.
7. Do not infer efficacy from licensing or professional regulation.
8. Confirm every internal link against the production build.
9. Assign a unique free, non-AI featured visual following the Kazakhstan visual policy.
10. Add publication, update and review dates only at release.

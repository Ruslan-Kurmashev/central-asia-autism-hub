## Provider-verified access and outcomes
- Both official course pages display **sign in and start learning**, course code, original language, lesson count and syllabus.
- https://mooc.orleu.edu.kz/courses/ lists each course as free.
- https://mooc.orleu.edu.kz/about/ states registration, lessons and completion documents are free; the platform provides a course-completion document. **It does not publish specific assessment thresholds for either course on the public pages**, so none are invented in catalogue descriptions.
- No claim of professional accreditation, guaranteed individual enrollment, cross-border recognition or full linguistic homogeneity of every teaching element.
- A quality flag: the public syllabus for course 9 contains at least one unrelated description (finance/money) under communication module 4 and some Russian headings. Do not imply that every lesson was independently audited or that all syllabus fragments are exclusively Kazakh.
- The published pages are informational listings, not endorsements of all teaching materials by AutismHub.

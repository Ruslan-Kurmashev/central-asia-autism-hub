# Original genetics article illustrations

Prepared for the Russian-language draft "Аутизм и генетика: какую роль играет наследственность?", 15 October 2026.

Both SVG files were written as editable, self-hosted vector markup specifically for AutismHub. No stock photos, generative image models, publisher figures, patient images, protected medical graphics, commercial marks or actual genetic test data were used.

| Filename | Use | Scientific limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `autism-genetics-overview-cover.svg` | Conceptual DNA double-helix cover. | Not a measured genomic sequence or example of a specific variant associated with autism. |
| `autism-genetics-variant-types.svg` | Difference between many common variants of small average statistical effect and some rare variants of larger effect; independently distinguishes inherited and de novo variants. | Drawn sizes and symbols do not convey prevalences, effect sizes, risk percentages or personal diagnoses. Not all rare variants are harmful. |

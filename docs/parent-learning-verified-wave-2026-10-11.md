### Editorial findings
- IRAV: free online course, 13 videos, 3-month access; electronic participation certificate on completion, all confirmed at the named course URL.
- Caritas: 17 modules, email registration, named certificate after final assessment; confirmed by official course FAQ and registration form.
- Nine Steps: downloadable facilitator and participant materials; only list these **materials**, not an on-demand nine-session group training or a certificate.
- Autism Journal lecture: the publicly available first lecture only; not a whole course or certificate.
- Help Is in Your Hands: four online coaching modules via account; only promise access to videos and home strategies, not a certificate.
- ADEPT: two visible sets of ten lessons and linked forms; not an unverified certificate.
- Foundations of Evidence-Based Strategies (Toddler and Preschool Age): free account and course completion certificate; optional CE credits depend on participating bodies.
- About Autism in Toddlers: materials can be reviewed without enrolment; EITA 3-hour certificate requires quiz and reflection.
- Autism NI: free on-demand webinar for parents/carers, form booking and subsequent emailed link. **Do not promise enrolment from any particular country or a certificate**.

### Quality safeguards
All summaries state course content, verified initial access steps, and verified learning materials / outcomes. Certificate descriptions are included **only** where the issuer explicitly confirms them. Providers may change availability and course policies. A published listing is not a guarantee that an individual can complete registration from a specific country. University names remain in official original form. Distinguish courses, downloadable materials, an individual lecture, and an on-demand webinar instead of calling all of them courses.

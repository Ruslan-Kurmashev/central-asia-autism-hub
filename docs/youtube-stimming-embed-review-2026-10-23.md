# Single YouTube video embed: privacy and accessibility review

Date: 2026-10-23

## Purpose and scope

A parent explicitly selected this third-party hand-flapping example for viewing on the AutismHub article page. This is a single editorial illustration, not a diagnostic demonstration, endorsement, clinical guideline, or part of a screening test. The original video is embedded from YouTube, not downloaded or rehosted.

## Visitor data and controls

Before the visitor activates the video, the article shows a local HTML button and text only. It loads no YouTube thumbnail, iframe or tracking library. On activation, a player is created under `www.youtube-nocookie.com`, YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. The external provider may then receive technical data including the requester's IP address, browser information and referrer origin and may process data under its own policies. AutismHub does not control provider-side processing. A direct link offers a fallback when the embed is blocked or JavaScript is disabled. Clicking that link also takes the visitor to YouTube.

The site's public privacy summary has been updated in Russian, Kazakh and English. No cookies, analytics, user accounts or additional third-party scripts were added to the site.

## Accessibility and editorial limitations

The visible button is keyboard-operable, has an accessible name, and does not autoplay video or audio. The iframe has a title and a responsive 16:9 container. A text description and direct link remain readable without JavaScript. The site does not claim third-party captions or transcripts are available. The video cannot establish the depicted child's diagnosis, emotional state or reason for moving their hands.

The clip is publicly referenced by the University of New Mexico in educational slides about hand flapping (https://cdd.health.unm.edu/ecln/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/FIT-Annual-Meeting.PPT-ECEP_ASD-and-SPIKES.pdf). The uploader's parental permission, accessibility features, continued availability and embed setting have not been independently confirmed. A link fallback is retained. If the rights holder raises an objection, remove the embed. Follow YouTube's documented embed and privacy-enhanced mode guidance: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780

This is an implementation review, not an independent legal compliance assessment.

## In brief

When planning a child's schooling, distinguish three questions: **how the child is enrolled**, **which special educational needs they have**, and **what adjustments or additional support they require**.

These are related but separate matters. They should not be determined automatically by an autism diagnosis alone.

## Starting Grade 1 in the 2026-2027 academic year

According to [gov.kz](https://www.gov.kz/situations/16/80), the national first-grade admission campaign for the **2026-2027 academic year** ran from **27 May to 31 August 2026**, but applications opened [in phases by region](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/tmr-roo/press/news/details/1225580?lang=ru): 27 May (Astana and specified regions), 10 June (Almaty city and another group), 18 June (Shymkent and another group), and 29 June (remaining specified regions). Parents could apply through the OQU education platform using eGov.kz or eGov Mobile authorisation, or directly at their school. State school admission also depends on the school catchment area and allocation of places. A request for a school outside the catchment area does not by itself guarantee a place. This was the schedule for **2026 only**, do not use these dates to plan applications in subsequent years.

Under the admission rules, children who are six years old by 1 September are enrolled regardless of their level of academic preparation. Where a child turns six **after** 1 September, admission may take place at the request of their parent or legal representative. Other enrolment situations and transfers have their own procedures and should be checked separately.

Official guidance states that a school should not require documents beyond the prescribed list. If you are asked to meet an additional condition, request a reference to the specific rule and clarify the matter with the local education authority.

## Admission and educational support are not the same thing

Admission rules establish how a child enters school. A separate process may then be needed to determine individual educational adjustments and psychological and pedagogical support.

It is useful for the family to prepare examples of **what helps the child learn, communicate and participate** before meeting school staff.

## What is the role of PMPK?

Under [Order No. 4](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2200026618), a school can identify initial learning difficulties. Its psychological and pedagogical support service (SPPS) may carry out a more detailed assessment with parental consent. PMPK, Kazakhstan's psychological, medical and pedagogical consultation service, assesses special educational needs when a child has substantial learning difficulties, either at a parent's initiative or following the school's recommendation.

PMPK recommendations inform particular specialised support arrangements for children with disabilities. However, not every request for help at school requires a PMPK conclusion first. Ask the school which measures can be arranged through its own assessment and which require PMPK recommendations.

Read a PMPK conclusion for the specific educational needs, recommended conditions and any required reassessment. PMPK assessment does not replace a medical autism diagnosis.

## What does the school support service do?

Rules governing the school-based **psychological and pedagogical support service** were approved by **Order No. 92 of 29 April 2025** and amended by **Order No. 144-NQ of 29 May 2026**.

The earlier **Order No. 6 of 12 January 2022** is no longer in force and should not be presented as the applicable set of rules.

The rules address identifying difficulties, assessing needs and working with families. Where indicated, the school support service develops an individual psychological and pedagogical support plan (IPPS), agrees it with parents and reviews progress. In the relevant pathway, the plan may run for one to three school terms before progress and the need for further support are assessed.

Psychological assessment, individual or group psychological counselling and training covered by the school support rules require written consent from parents or legal representatives. This is not automatically permission to share a child's photographs or recordings.

For behavioural difficulties and problems adapting to class, the school support service may arrange individual support from a teaching assistant for one school term. PMPK determines whether this assistance is needed beyond that period. A teaching assistant is not automatically assigned to every autistic child.

## What adjustments can families discuss?

Depending on the child, adjustments might include:

- a predictable visual structure and timetable
- accessible communication, including **augmentative and alternative communication (AAC)**
- presenting instructions in a different way
- extra time
- sensory adjustments
- support when moving between lessons
- support during breaks and school activities
- work towards clear learning and functional goals.

**Not every child needs every adjustment.** The aim is to agree on measures that address observed barriers to participation.

## If the school refuses admission or does not organise support

Ask for a **written explanation** of the decision and the relevant legal basis. Keep copies of your application, the school's responses and any PMPK recommendations.

If the issue cannot be resolved directly with the school, contact your **local education authority** or use an official government complaints channel.

Cases should be assessed individually. An organisational difficulty is not automatically the same as an unlawful refusal to enrol, but an unexplained verbal refusal should not be the only response a family receives.

## Home education is a separate question

**Education at home for medical reasons is not an automatic consequence of autism.** Under [Order No. 385](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2200029329), free individual education at home is arranged for children who cannot attend school for a prolonged period on medical grounds, supported by a conclusion of the medical advisory commission (VKK).

PMPK assesses educational needs but its recommendation does not replace the VKK medical conclusion. If home education is proposed solely because of an autism diagnosis or difficulty settling in class, ask the school for the specific legal and medical grounds. A transfer from a general school to a special education organisation requires parental consent and PMPK conclusions and recommendations.

## Questions to ask the school

1. Who is part of the psychological and pedagogical support team, and how can the family request school-based assessment before seeking PMPK advice?
2. Who is the family's main contact?
3. Which special educational needs have been identified?
4. Which adjustments can the school actually provide?
5. How will measurable goals be formulated?
6. Is an individual support plan needed, who agrees it with parents and when will it be reviewed?
7. What is the school's response to sensory overload, bullying, leaving the classroom unexpectedly or other risks?
8. How can the child communicate pain, anxiety or a need for a break?

## Your next step

Before meeting school staff, prepare a short **child profile**: strengths, preferred communication methods, main difficulties, sensory needs, safety considerations and **three to five conditions** that help the child learn and take part.

If you are still considering preschool, see [Autism and kindergarten in Kazakhstan: enrolment and support](/central-asia-autism-hub/kz/en/help-kazakhstan/autism-kindergarten-support-kazakhstan/).

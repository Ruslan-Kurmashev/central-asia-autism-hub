## In brief

After an autism diagnosis, families often ask: "What benefits does a child with autism qualify for?"

A more reliable question is: **What legal status has been established, which social protection measures have been identified and which government services apply to this particular family's circumstances?**

A diagnosis and entitlement to a specific benefit or service are **not the same thing**.

## Where to start: three different questions

| Your question | Main pathway |
| --- | --- |
| Do we need a medical assessment and possible referral for disability evaluation? | Treating clinician and the medical organisation's medical advisory commission (**VKK**) |
| Do we need determination of disability status and individual support measures? | **Medical and social expert assessment (MSE)** under the applicable rules |
| Do we need benefits or specialised social services? | The relevant government service, depending on the legal status, documents and eligibility conditions |

**An autism spectrum disorder (ASD) diagnosis does not, by itself, establish disability status** or guarantee any particular benefit.

Healthcare and education processes can take place in parallel, but **PMPK**, Kazakhstan's psychological, medical and pedagogical consultation service, does not replace MSE.

## MSE and disability determination

**Medical and social expert assessment (MSE)** follows specific rules and is used to determine disability-related questions and necessary social protection measures.

Relevant medical information and other documents may be taken into account. For a child, a **PMPK conclusion, if available**, can be among the information considered in certain circumstances.

Families should not be promised a particular disability classification, duration of status or set of support measures solely on the basis of an autism diagnosis.

The MSE rules were updated by **Order No. 329 of 11 August 2026**, with changes effective from **25 August 2026**. The precise information and documents required depend on the circumstances. The PMPK conclusion is considered **when available**, not as a universal mandatory document for every application.

## What is IPAR?

**IPAR** is the individual rehabilitation and habilitation plan for a person with a disability (Kazakhstani Russian abbreviation: `ИПАР`).

It identifies particular measures for rehabilitation, habilitation and support.

When considering a service, check whether it is included in the person's IPAR and what additional eligibility or application requirements apply.

## Which documents and details should be checked after MSE?

Confirm whether:

- a formal MSE decision has been issued;
- disability status has been established;
- any relevant duration or review date has been specified;
- the necessary measures are included in the IPAR.

If the decision contains an error or unclear wording, seek clarification through the authorised agency and the prescribed procedure.

Also check which measures may be arranged **proactively** and which require an individual application. Avoid sending scans of personal documents to private intermediaries without checking whether they are needed and how the information will be handled.

## State benefits

Kazakhstan's **Social Code** provides for state social benefits and a separate allowance for a person bringing up a child with a disability.

In particular, distinguish:

- the **state social disability benefit**; and
- the **allowance for a person raising a child with a disability**.

These are **different grounds for entitlement and different award procedures**, reflected in Article 170 of the Social Code.

Benefit amounts may change when statutory calculation indices or the law change. AutismHub therefore does not present a single undated amount as permanently valid. Any published figure should be verified against official sources and labelled with the date of the check.

## Specialised social services

**Specialised social services** may be provided in different settings, including residential, day-care or other semi-residential arrangements, and support delivered at home.

This is a distinct part of the social protection system. Not every private autism intervention programme is a "specialised social service" within the legal meaning of that term.

Eligibility, assessed needs, the service provider and regional availability matter when considering a particular form of support.

## Where to find reliable information

Use the current versions of Kazakhstan's [Social Code](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2300000224) and [MSE rules](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2300032922). For an application, follow the relevant government service page on **eGov.kz** or **gov.kz**.

Verify the requirements immediately before applying. Some legal amendments have **delayed commencement dates, including provisions scheduled to take effect in 2027**; they must not be treated as already in force.

For specialised social services, identify the specific format needed, such as support at home, semi-residential support or residential services, and check eligibility and local availability.

## A checklist for families

1. Which **official status** has already been established?
2. Which documents were issued after MSE?
3. Is there an IPAR, and which measures are included?
4. Which **specific government service** does the family need?
5. Where can it be requested?
6. Which information can the government system retrieve automatically, and which documents must be supplied?
7. Are there regional conditions, limited places or a waiting list?

## Why there is no single universal list of "autism benefits"

Eligibility may depend on the child's age, disability status, IPAR, family circumstances, the particular service and the region.

A list without these qualifications can quickly become outdated or create misleading expectations.

## Your next step

If the family is only beginning to consider disability assessment, start by discussing the **VKK and possible MSE pathway** with the relevant healthcare provider.

If disability status has already been established, review the **IPAR** and identify the specific government services that apply.

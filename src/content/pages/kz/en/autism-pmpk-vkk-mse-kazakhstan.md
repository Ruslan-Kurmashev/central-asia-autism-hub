After a child's diagnosis, parents may encounter several abbreviations: PMPK, VKK and MSE. It may seem as though they are consecutive stages of one large commission. In practice, they are different procedures that answer different questions.

**A psychiatrist, PMPK, VKK and MSE are not interchangeable.** One document should not automatically be considered a substitute for another.

For an explanation of the medical diagnostic process, see [How is autism diagnosed in a child in Kazakhstan?](../how-autism-is-diagnosed-in-kazakhstan/).

## The essentials in one minute

| Service or professional | Main question | What the family receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Psychiatrist | Which medical diagnosis fits the clinical picture? | Medical report, diagnosis and recommendations |
| PMPK | What special educational needs and learning conditions does the child have? | PMPK conclusion and recommendations |
| VKK | Is a formal medical commission decision required? | VKK conclusion |
| MSE | Are disability criteria met, considering persistent functional impairments and limitations in everyday activities? | MSE expert decision |

More than one pathway may be relevant for one child. Another family may need only a medical diagnosis or a decision about educational support.

Rather than starting with "Which commission must we attend?", ask:

> **What issue are we trying to resolve now?**

## Who establishes a medical ASD diagnosis?

Medical diagnosis belongs to the healthcare system.

Kazakhstan's Healthcare Code states that a psychiatrist establishes a diagnosis of a mental or behavioural disorder according to the criteria of the international classification of diseases. Primary healthcare physicians have separate, limited authority to diagnose and treat specific borderline mental and behavioural disorders within a scope set by the authorised agency.

The published mental healthcare standard lists a limited set of such disorders that primary care may diagnose independently. ASD, classified under F84 in the system described, is not on that list.

Medical ASD diagnosis therefore should not be replaced with the conclusion of an educational or social commission.

If a family needs to know whether a medical ASD diagnosis has been made, what code applies and how the diagnostic assessment takes place, this belongs to the psychiatric pathway.

Read [How is autism diagnosed in a child in Kazakhstan?](../how-autism-is-diagnosed-in-kazakhstan/).

## What is PMPK?

PMPK stands for *psychological-medical-pedagogical consultation*.

PMPK belongs to the education system. Its remit extends beyond a specific medical diagnosis. It conducts a multidisciplinary assessment and determines a child's special educational needs.

The published rules provide for various professionals to participate in PMPK, including a neurologist, psychiatrist, psychologist, speech and language specialist, special education teacher and social educator. The sequence and scope of assessment depend on the child's circumstances.

Initial assessment includes collecting developmental information, evaluating psychological, speech-language and social-communication characteristics, professional assessments, determining special educational needs and advising parents.

## What can PMPK recommend?

Following an assessment, PMPK prepares an overall conclusion and recommendations.

Depending on the child's needs, these may concern:

- the educational programme
- ways to assess learning outcomes
- specialised or alternative teaching methods
- the form of education
- adaptation of the learning environment
- special technical and assistive devices
- psychological and educational support
- support from a teacher's assistant
- social and educational assistance.

Parents receive PMPK findings and recommendations indicating the child's special educational needs and type of educational programme.

The main PMPK question is:

> **What learning conditions and support does this child need to learn and develop?**

## Can PMPK play a part in diagnosis?

Yes, but it is important not to confuse different documents.

A psychiatrist is included among PMPK professionals. Psychiatric assessment there may help evaluate mental health, psychological and language development and identify mental disorders. An overall PMPK conclusion may also include a collective decision about the type of developmental impairment.

Saying that "PMPK has absolutely nothing to do with diagnosis" would therefore be an oversimplification.

However, the PMPK conclusion primarily serves an educational purpose. It should not automatically be treated as a replacement for healthcare documentation produced after psychiatric diagnosis.

Practical takeaway:

> If a PMPK report refers to a developmental condition associated with autism but you need an actual medical diagnosis, clarify which separate medical report exists and who issued it.

## Must a diagnosis be obtained before going to PMPK?

Not always.

PMPK may be needed because a child has difficulties with development, speech, behaviour, learning or adaptation and their special educational needs need to be assessed.

Medical and educational pathways can operate in parallel:

**medical assessment -> answers the medical question**

**PMPK -> answers the educational question**

There is no universal rule requiring every family to complete the full medical pathway before contacting PMPK.

## What is VKK?

VKK is a *medical advisory commission* within a healthcare organisation.

It makes formal medical decisions within its assigned authority. VKK is not a special "autism commission", and it is not required merely to make a medical ASD diagnosis "official".

The published rules include timely, justified referral to medical-social expertise among VKK functions, alongside oversight of the timing and quality of medical documentation for such referrals.

VKK becomes relevant when there is a separate medical or administrative question that requires a commission decision.

## Is VKK required to confirm autism?

There is no universal requirement to attend VKK solely to confirm an already established medical ASD diagnosis.

VKK and psychiatric diagnosis serve different functions:

**psychiatrist -> clinical diagnosis**

**VKK -> formal medical commission decisions in specified circumstances**

A particularly relevant VKK role for families is deciding whether to refer a child for MSE.

## What is MSE?

MSE stands for *medical-social expertise*.

It serves a different purpose from both a psychiatrist and PMPK. MSE assesses not merely the name of a condition but the persistence of functional impairments, limitations on everyday activities and eligibility for a disability determination.

The version of the MSE rules discussed here describes limitations in everyday activities in terms of partial or complete loss of the ability or opportunity to care for oneself, move, orient oneself, communicate, control behaviour, learn or work.

An MSE decision is made on the basis of an integrated assessment of bodily functions and the degree of activity limitations, using the applicable classifications of impairments and limitations.

The central MSE question is not simply:

> "Does the child have an ASD diagnosis?"

It is instead:

> **Are there persistent functional impairments and activity limitations that meet the established disability criteria?**

## An ASD diagnosis and disability status are not the same

A medical diagnosis addresses a health condition and whether the clinical picture meets diagnostic criteria.

MSE addresses a different question: whether persistent functional impairments and limitations justify disability status.

An ASD diagnosis alone therefore does not automatically confer disability status.

Two children with the same diagnostic code may differ substantially in communication, independence, learning, behaviour and support needs. This is why MSE examines the child's functioning as well as the diagnosis.

## How is a child referred to MSE?

The version of the MSE rules described here provides for assessment following a referral from the VKK of a healthcare organisation at the place of permanent registration.

In simplified terms:

**healthcare organisation -> necessary diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation measures -> VKK -> MSE referral -> MSE expert decision**

The basis for MSE is a VKK referral in the presence of persistent functional impairment or when reassessment is needed.

The VKK chair of the referring healthcare organisation oversees the completeness of examination, justification for referral, timing and quality of medical documents.

## What is form No. 031/u?

When referring a patient to MSE, the healthcare organisation completes **form No. 031/u, "Conclusion for medical-social expertise"**.

The form is certified using the electronic digital signature of the VKK chair or their substitute. The version of the MSE rules described here also establishes a validity period of no more than one month from the date of signature.

Parents do not need to prepare this form themselves. If MSE referral is being considered, ask the healthcare organisation whether form No. 031/u has been completed and what other documents are needed for the particular case.

## Is it necessary to wait four months after diagnosis?

For children, the version of the MSE rules discussed here sets a general rule: a healthcare organisation refers a child for MSE after diagnostic, treatment and rehabilitation measures confirming persistent functional impairment, **no earlier than four months after diagnosis**.

The rules provide exceptions for particular conditions, including anatomical defects, trisomy 21 and certain incurable conditions with significant or profound functional impairment and no rehabilitation potential.

It would therefore be wrong to turn the four-month rule into "every child must wait exactly four months under all circumstances".

The healthcare organisation determines referral timing for an individual case according to the rules in force on the date of contact.

## Is PMPK required for MSE?

**A PMPK conclusion is not a universally mandatory document for every child referred to MSE.**

In the version of the MSE rules described here, the PMPK conclusion for the initial or repeat assessment of children is listed **if available**.

The primary medical basis for MSE is the healthcare organisation's VKK referral.

If the child has already attended PMPK, its findings may provide important information on educational needs and functioning and can be included in the evidence for MSE.

A practical question for the healthcare organisation:

> **Which documents are required in our specific case to refer our child to MSE?**

This is more useful than gathering as many certificates as possible without knowing their purpose.

## Does MSE diagnose autism?

MSE does not replace clinical ASD diagnosis.

During the expert assessment, professionals use medical documentation, specialist opinions, investigation results and information about the child's functioning. In specific cases, relevant specialists may be consulted to clarify the diagnosis or the extent of functional impairments recorded on form No. 031/u.

The primary task of MSE nevertheless remains expert evaluation of activity limitations and disability status, not the psychiatrist's ordinary clinical diagnosis of ASD.

## Which document does your family need?

| Your current task | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Determine whether there is a medical ASD diagnosis | Psychiatrist |
| Identify special educational needs | PMPK |
| Obtain recommendations on the educational programme or special learning conditions | PMPK |
| Obtain a formal medical commission decision | VKK |
| Healthcare organisation considering referring a child to MSE | VKK |
| Obtain a decision regarding disability status | MSE following referral under the established procedure |

You do not need to attend all four institutions just because a child has, or may have, ASD. The appropriate route depends on the family's current question.

## Which documents do not replace each other?

| Document | What it is used for | What it does not replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Psychiatrist's medical report | Medical diagnosis | PMPK conclusion or MSE expert decision |
| PMPK conclusion | Educational needs and recommendations | Psychiatric healthcare documentation |
| VKK conclusion | Formal medical commission decision | MSE expert decision |
| MSE decision | Result of medical-social expertise | Medical diagnosis or educational recommendations |

Keep these documents organised separately in the child's folder, since they serve different pathways and may be needed for different purposes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Our child has an ASD diagnosis. Do we need PMPK?

If the family needs to establish the educational programme, special learning conditions, psychological-educational support or other special educational needs, PMPK may be relevant.

A medical diagnosis alone does not automatically describe all the child's educational needs.

### We have attended PMPK. Must we also see a psychiatrist?

It depends on what issue needs to be addressed.

If the family requires a medical diagnosis, follow-up healthcare or decisions on other medical questions, the PMPK report should not automatically replace the medical pathway.

### A psychiatrist diagnosed ASD. Is the child automatically granted disability status?

No. MSE assesses persistent functional impairment and activity limitations, not merely the diagnostic code.

### Does VKK have to confirm ASD?

No. VKK handles separate medical commission questions. It is not a universally mandatory step for confirming an ASD medical diagnosis.

### Can we go directly to MSE with a psychiatrist's report?

The ordinary regulatory pathway involves a referral to MSE through a healthcare organisation's VKK. A psychiatrist's report alone does not replace the established referral procedure.

### Is PMPK mandatory before MSE?

There is no universal requirement to have a PMPK conclusion in every case. The MSE rules refer to it for children **if available**.

## There is no mandatory chain of four commissions

It is misleading to describe every child's pathway as:

**psychiatrist -> PMPK -> VKK -> MSE**

This suggests all four stages are required for every family.

It is more accurate to separate the questions:

**Need a medical diagnosis?**

-> psychiatrist

**Need special educational needs assessed?**

-> PMPK

**Need a formal medical commission decision?**

-> VKK

**Need to resolve disability status?**

-> healthcare organisation and VKK -> MSE.

## What to remember

**A psychiatrist** is responsible for medical psychiatric diagnosis.

**PMPK** assesses special educational needs and recommends necessary learning conditions and psychological-educational support.

**VKK** makes formal medical commission decisions and, when justified, refers the patient to MSE.

**MSE** assesses persistent impairments in bodily functions and activity limitations and makes an expert decision about disability status.

If you are unsure which service to approach, identify the specific question first: medical diagnosis, educational support, a medical commission decision or disability status.

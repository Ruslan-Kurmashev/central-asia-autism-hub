## 1. Purpose of the recommendations

HAS describes the pathway from initial parental or professional concerns to specialist diagnostic assessment. The document aims to reduce unjustified delays, coordinate professional actions and make the pathway clearer for families.

The document **does not address selection of a particular therapy**. Interventions are covered by other HAS recommendations updated in 2026.

## 2. Warning signs

Detection may consider reciprocal communication and social interaction, repetitive actions, restricted interests and the child's developmental history.

The documents pay particular attention to parental views and loss of previously acquired skills. These signs warrant professional assessment, but none individually establishes ASD.

### Age-related guidance in HAS 2018

| Age | Reason for detailed assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Any | Parental concerns about development, especially communication and language |
| Any | Loss of language or social skills |
| From 12 months | Absence of babbling, distal pointing or other communicative gestures |
| From 18 months | No words |
| From 24 months | No spontaneous word combinations other than echolalia |

Regression requires medical assessment, particularly when neurological signs are present. These indicators are not independent ASD criteria. HAS also highlights the possibility of later recognition of autism in girls.

[Full HAS 2018 text, section 1.1](https://www.has-sante.fr/upload/docs/application/pdf/2018-02/trouble_du_spectre_de_lautisme_de_lenfant_et_ladolescent__recommandations.pdf).

## 3. Primary and specialist stages

| Stage | Task | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First line | Discuss signs, developmental history, clinical concerns and referral | Does not replace specialist diagnosis |
| Second line | Comprehensive assessment of development, communication, functioning and co-occurring conditions | Not reducible to one test or score |
| Third line | Expert assistance in complex or unclear cases | Not required for every child |
| Feedback | Explain findings, limitations and next steps to the family | Should not delay available support |

This division is **an organisational arrangement of the French system**. It does not create identical legal categories of professional roles and services in Kazakhstan.

### HAS 2018 pathway timescales

Following the initial medical consultation about warning signs, a dedicated primary care consultation should be arranged within three weeks. When ASD risk is confirmed, the child is referred immediately to second-line specialists. Initial investigations and needs-based support are organised in parallel. The target for starting necessary interventions in a young child is less than three months after identifying developmental concerns. If detection findings are uncertain, repeat detailed assessment is provided within one month. If risk is not confirmed, surveillance continues. These are French guideline targets, not deadlines established by Kazakhstan legislation.

## 4. Instruments and clinical judgement

HAS gives examples of observation and standardised tools according to age and assessment purpose. A positive screen is not a diagnostic conclusion, and a negative screen does not always remove clinical suspicion.

Professional diagnosis must consider developmental history, information across settings and other possible explanations for difficulties.

| Instrument | Context in the 2018 document |
| --- | --- |
| M-CHAT with Follow-Up | 16-30 months, original version with follow-up interview |
| SCQ | After 48 months, accounting for developmental level |
| ASSQ, AQ, SRS-2 | Examples for children and adolescents without co-occurring intellectual disability, accounting for version and age |

Age ranges and instrument versions must be checked against current developer instructions. HAS 2018 separately includes M-CHAT-R/F in Appendix 3, so the age range of the original M-CHAT cannot automatically be applied to every version. No questionnaire replaces clinical assessment.

### Medical and differential assessment

HAS provides for medical examination, hearing and vision assessment and consideration of genetic consultation and neurological assessment where indicated. EEG, MRI and additional laboratory investigations are not mandatory tests for establishing ASD. Their use depends on clinical findings, particularly suspicion of a co-occurring condition or an alternative explanation for symptoms. Aetiological assessment approaches should be checked against current specialist recommendations.

## 5. Family and early support

The recommendations emphasise listening to parents and agreeing next steps. When developmental needs have already been identified, discuss appropriate support promptly rather than treating the wait for a definitive conclusion as an automatic obstacle.

Do not promise a cure for autism as a result of early intervention or derive an individual prognosis from age at detection.

## 6. Difference from the new HAS recommendations

| Document | Content |
| --- | --- |
| HAS 2018 | Signs, detection, diagnostic assessment |
| HAS 2026 | Individualised interventions and life pathways for children and adolescents |

These are two separate primary sources. A separate update date for interventions **is not** the update date for diagnostic guidance.

## 7. Applicability in Kazakhstan

Use the document to compare the quality of referral pathways, clinical assessment and communication with families. Do not transfer French payment arrangements, licences, professional authority or specific report formats without checking Kazakhstan legislation.

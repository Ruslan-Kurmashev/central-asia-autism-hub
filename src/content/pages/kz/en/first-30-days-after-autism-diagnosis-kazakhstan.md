After an ASD diagnosis, families do not need to visit every specialist or complete every document within one month. A more useful starting point is to identify what the child needs now and seek available support. Help with communication, daily activities or adaptation can be discussed without waiting for every administrative procedure to finish.

The week-by-week outline below is **a family planning guide, not a compulsory sequence or a legal deadline**. If medical or educational support is needed sooner, do not wait for the suggested week.

## Days 1-3. Obtain the report and clarify medical advice

**What to do**

1. Keep the medical report, assessment results and recommendations securely. Before giving copies to a preschool, school or centre, ask which information is necessary for that purpose. Do not share medical documents in group chats.
2. If recommendations are unclear, ask the doctor who diagnosed the child to explain them in plain language. Clarify whether a follow-up, monitoring or referral is needed.
3. For general health, development or services linked to your local healthcare provider, consult your paediatrician or primary care doctor. Ask whether a child development and early intervention centre (ЦРРВ) is available.

**Why:** to understand what was actually recommended and which questions remain open.

**Outcome:** you have the medical report, recommendations and the next medical step if one is indicated.

If you received test results but are unsure whether a diagnosis has been made, first read [Understanding autism assessment results in Kazakhstan](../understanding-autism-assessment-results-in-kazakhstan/).

## Week one. Choose one or two immediate priorities

Rather than listing every characteristic of the child, note what most affects their health, communication, learning or everyday life right now.

| Current concern | Possible first step |
| --- | --- |
| The child finds it difficult to express wants or needs | Discuss communication support with a qualified professional, including gestures, pictures or other accessible methods |
| Difficulties arise at preschool or school | Contact teachers and the psychological and educational support service (СППС) |
| Sleep problems, pain, feeding difficulties or other symptoms | Consult a doctor to assess possible medical causes |

**What to do:** choose one or two priorities and describe the help you hope to receive. For example, "help the child request water" or "find out how to reduce overload at preschool".

**Outcome:** you know which issue to raise first and with whom.

Do not assume every new symptom is caused by autism. Loss of acquired skills, possible seizures, serious feeding problems or other new health symptoms need timely clinical assessment. Seek emergency medical help for an immediate threat to health or safety rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

## Weeks one to two. Decide on the educational support route

The appropriate route depends on where the child needs help.

### If the child attends preschool or school

1. Tell the teacher or administration which difficulties arise in learning, communication or everyday participation.
2. Ask for a meeting with the psychological and educational support service (СППС) and discuss an assessment of special educational needs.
3. Ask which measures the institution can begin itself and whether a PMPK conclusion is needed.

A PMPK conclusion is not automatically required for every support measure at preschool or school. It is, however, a basis for certain forms of special psychological and educational assistance.

### If a PMPK assessment is needed

The psychological, medical and pedagogical consultation (PMPK) assesses special educational needs and recommends appropriate learning conditions and support. A PMPK conclusion may be necessary for certain special services, including a psychological and educational correction office (КППК), or to resolve educational questions.

**How to apply**

1. Open the [official government service page](https://www.gov.kz/services/4292?lang=ru) and locate the service for psychological, medical and pedagogical assessment and consultation.
2. Apply directly to PMPK or through the digital government portal. The rules also allow an application using a mobile device registered on the portal. Keep confirmation of online submission.
3. Check the current document list. The child's identity document is usually needed. Prepare existing medical reports, but do not arrange extra investigations without a separate reason. PMPK may request more materials for an in-depth assessment.
4. Keep the assessment appointment date. After the assessment, obtain the conclusion and ask where to take it next.

The PMPK service is free. Under the [2026 version of the state-service rules](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020744), the **waiting period for an assessment is up to 30 calendar days**. For an in-person request the appointment date is set on the day of application. Through the portal or proactive route it is set within **two working days**. These are different timings: receiving a date does not mean the assessment happens that day, and the assessment waiting limit does not guarantee the start of sessions at a correction centre. The 90-365-day periods shown for other services on [gov.kz](https://www.gov.kz/services/4292?lang=ru) are not the waiting period for the PMPK assessment conclusion.

**Outcome:** you have discussed support with the school or preschool and, where relevant, submitted a PMPK request, received an appointment date or obtained a conclusion. PMPK is not a compulsory step during the first month when the child's needs do not require it.

## Weeks two to three. Arrange available support

After consultations or recommendations, find out what is available locally. Depending on the child's needs, this may include school professionals, a correction office, a rehabilitation centre, an autism support centre or a medical service operating within its remit.

**What to do**

1. If you have a PMPK conclusion, contact the recommended organisation about admission, required documents, vacancies and waiting lists. The conclusion does not guarantee immediate enrolment.
2. If assistance is not yet available, ask how your application or waiting-list place is recorded, who can advise you meanwhile, and keep the date and confirmation.
3. When choosing a professional, ask about a specific goal, their approach to the child, qualifications, safety and how meaningful changes in everyday life will be assessed.

While waiting, families can support the child's familiar communication methods, offer accessible choices, take account of sensitivity to light and noise and make routines more predictable. Respect the child's preferences and consent. Do not force eye contact or require a child to tolerate obviously distressing sensory input. These general measures are not a treatment programme for ASD or a replacement for individual assessment.

**Outcome:** you know where support has been requested, whether there is a waiting list, who is responsible for follow-up and one useful everyday adjustment you can already make.

See also [How to choose a specialist and support for an autistic child](/central-asia-autism-hub/kz/ru/parents/kak-vybrat-spetsialista-i-pomoshch-pri-autizme/). This related resource is currently available in Russian.

## Weeks three to four. Clarify social support issues if relevant

An ASD diagnosis does not automatically establish disability status. Medical and social expertise (MSE) assesses persistent impairment and limitations in daily functioning. Referral is arranged by a healthcare organisation through its medical advisory commission (VKK) under the prescribed procedure.

Under the [MSE rules amended with effect from 2 November 2026](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2600040183), the general condition for referring children is completion of necessary diagnostic, treatment and rehabilitation measures confirming persistent impairment, usually **no earlier than four months after the diagnosis was established**. The rules contain exceptions for specified conditions. Therefore, obtaining an MSE determination is not a compulsory or guaranteed step during the first 30 days. Consent to the processing of personal and medical information is also provided for in the MSE process.

**What to do:** if your child has substantial functional limitations, ask the treating doctor whether there are grounds to consider VKK referral and MSE, and which timing applies in your case. If it is not relevant now, there is no need to pursue MSE solely because of the diagnosis.

**Outcome:** you know whether to discuss disability assessment now or return to the question later.

The roles of the services are explained in [Psychiatrist, PMPK, VKK and MSE: who does what in Kazakhstan](../psychiatrist-pmpk-vkk-mse-kazakhstan/).

Do not assume every child needs MRI, EEG, genetic investigations and laboratory tests. Additional tests should address specific medical questions and are not universally required to "confirm" autism. See [Investigations for suspected autism in Kazakhstan](../tests-and-assessments-for-suspected-autism-in-kazakhstan/).

## Support for parents and the family

After a diagnosis, families may need time to understand the information, explain the situation to relatives and organise daily life. One person does not have to do everything alone.

If helpful, agree on who can assist with appointments, household tasks or care for other children. Ask local professionals about available support groups and services. International [NICE CG170 guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg170/chapter/recommendations) explicitly recognises the needs of parents, carers and other family members. Its service arrangements are not guaranteed entitlements in Kazakhstan.

This is not another mandatory item in the 30-day plan. Parents' needs can be addressed alongside the child's needs.

## By day 30. Write a plan for the next two to three months

Summarise the next steps as **task -> service or professional -> action -> date or condition for follow-up**.

| Task | Next step | When to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communication support | Discuss accessible ways to communicate with a selected professional | After the agreed consultation |
| Preschool support | Contact the support team and clarify measures | On a date agreed with the institution |
| PMPK | Prepare for the appointed assessment or request appointment information | On the date given when applying |

These are examples, not compulsory tasks for every child.

**End-of-month outcome:** not every service has necessarily started, but the next steps and contacts are clear, applications are recorded and the family knows whom to contact.

For the overall pathway see [Starting autism support in Kazakhstan (Russian-language source)](/central-asia-autism-hub/kz/ru/help-kazakhstan/help-kazakhstan-start/). There are also resources on [preschool](/central-asia-autism-hub/kz/en/help-kazakhstan/autism-kindergarten-support-kazakhstan/), [school](/central-asia-autism-hub/kz/en/help-kazakhstan/autism-school-enrolment-support-kazakhstan/) and [social support](/central-asia-autism-hub/kz/en/help-kazakhstan/family-rights-social-support-kazakhstan/).

## Short checklist

- [ ] The medical report and recommendations are saved.
- [ ] It is clear whether another medical visit or evaluation of new symptoms is needed.
- [ ] One or two immediate needs have been identified.
- [ ] Where relevant, support has been discussed with preschool or school professionals.
- [ ] If PMPK is needed, the application procedure is known and an application has been made if appropriate.
- [ ] Available recommended support and waiting arrangements are understood.
- [ ] If relevant, VKK or MSE has been discussed with the treating doctor.
- [ ] The next actions, contacts and dates have been recorded.

If some steps are incomplete because of queues or local service availability, this does not mean the family has "failed" to complete the month. The aim is to know what happens next.
